Team Building & Outdoor Challenges in Eilat

Professional outdoor experiences for companies, organizations and teams — from ODT and team challenges to rappelling, rope activities and desert adventures.

Outdoor Team Building (ODT)

Take your team out of the office and into the field. Our ODT activities combine practical challenges, teamwork, communication, leadership and decision-making in an outdoor environment. The activities can be tailored to the size, goals and character of your organization.

ODT Workshops

Practical team missions designed to improve communication, cooperation, leadership and decision-making. Teams face unfamiliar outdoor challenges that require planning, coordination and creative problem-solving.

Raft Building

Teams receive basic materials and work together to design and build their own raft. After decorating their sails, the teams take their creations onto the water for a fun and competitive challenge.

Mission Race

Adventure races inspired by popular challenge formats, using maps, clues and field missions. Activities can also be combined with outdoor evening events, campfire gatherings and group games.
